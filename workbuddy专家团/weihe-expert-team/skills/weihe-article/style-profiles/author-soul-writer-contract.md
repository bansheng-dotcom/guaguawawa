# Author Soul Writer Contract

Use this as a light prewrite constraint for `section-writer`.

`author-soul.md` describes who is speaking. This contract only decides how the first draft should enter the section. It is not a paragraph checklist.

## Required Prewrite Choice

Before drafting, choose one primary author move for this section:

- enter from a real task, stuck point, failed output, workflow node, field, prompt, table, or validation problem
- expose one practical judgment or tradeoff the author would make
- keep one concrete anchor that can carry the section, such as a field, report item, prompt, table, small comparison, or verification step
- state a boundary only when the section is actually reaching a conclusion

Do not force every paragraph to contain all moves.

## Writer Contract Comment

In the HTML writer contract, include:

- `author_move`: the one primary author move chosen for this section
- `concrete_anchor`: the object that will carry the explanation
- `avoid_heavy_soul`: what not to add only for author flavor

## Do Not

- Do not open with encyclopedia definitions when the section has a usable task scene.
- Do not add a full scene, judgment, field action, boundary, and next step to every paragraph.
- Do not copy author catchphrases unless they carry a concrete judgment.
- Do not turn a short transition or table explanation into a complete mini-essay.
- Do not add new research claims or new business conclusions for style reasons.

## Good Direction

The draft should feel like it starts from a working problem, but it should stay light enough for review and rewrite nodes to improve it without having to undo a formula.
