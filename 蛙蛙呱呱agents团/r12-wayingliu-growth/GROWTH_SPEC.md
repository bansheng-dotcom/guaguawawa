# R12 Growth Specification Ledger (GROWTH_SPEC)
# Version: 1.0.0
# Domain: Growth Operations & RevOps (蛙引流)

## 1. North Star Metric
- Primary Metric: WAU (Weekly Active Users)
- Secondary Metric: Paid Retention Rate (D30 >= 20%)
- Core Equation: Growth = Traffic x Conversion Rate x Retention Rate x Virality (K-Factor)

## 2. Statistical Significance Standards
- P-Value Threshold: p < 0.05 (Double-tailed Z-test)
- Confidence Level: >= 95.0%
- Minimum Sample Size: 1000 users per variant
- Minimum MDE (Minimum Detectable Effect): 5.0%
- Power (1 - beta): >= 80.0%

## 3. Eight-Dimensional Growth Release Gates (8维增长门禁)
1. **Gate-01: Metric-Goal Alignment (北极星对齐门禁)**
   - All growth plans and campaigns must explicitly trace back to WAU or secondary metrics.
2. **Gate-02: Statistical Significance & Confidence (显著性门禁)**
   - A/B experiments and claims must provide valid p-value (<0.05) and 95% confidence interval.
3. **Gate-03: Unit Economics Health (单位经济学门禁)**
   - LTV/CAC ratio must be >= 3.0. Payback period must be <= 12 months.
4. **Gate-04: Virality & Referral Loop (病毒裂变门禁)**
   - K-Factor must be quantitatively estimated (K = Invites x Conversion).
5. **Gate-05: Omnichannel Compliance (全渠道合规门禁)**
   - Channels must adhere to platform standards (WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, AppStore, ColdEmail).
6. **Gate-06: Anti-Slop & Quantitative Evidence (去劣化与量化门禁)**
   - Prohibit vague buzzwords like '显著提升', '大幅增长', '待定'. Require quantitative actionability score >= 70.
7. **Gate-07: Scenario Matrix Coverage (三维方案决策门禁)**
   - Growth plans must evaluate 3 distinct scenarios: [Minimal Rapid / Steady Lean / Omnichannel ROI-Balanced].
8. **Gate-08: Redline & Anti-Fraud Safety (红线安全门禁)**
   - Strictly prohibit pure cash subsidy without ROI bounds, unauthorized scraping, or fake engagement.

## 4. Omnichannel Channel Standards (全渠道矩阵规范)
- **微信生态 (WeChat)**:
  - Format: Official Account / Mini Program / Channels
  - Minimum text: 800 chars, Maximum text: 5000 chars
  - CTR baseline: >= 1.5%
- **小红书 (Xiaohongshu)**:
  - Format: Seed Notes / Image Carousel
  - Minimum text: 200 chars, Maximum text: 1000 chars
  - Engagement baseline: >= 5.0%
- **付费投放 (Paid Media)**:
  - Target ROAS: >= 1.5
  - Max CAC: 200 CNY
- **冷邮件外联 (Cold Email)**:
  - Compliance: Secondary domain, Opt-out mechanism, Physical postal address
  - Reply rate target: >= 8.0%
- **应用商店 (ASO)**:
  - Rating target: >= 4.5
  - Keyword density: 2.0% - 5.0%
